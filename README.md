## :clapper: About

**Interstellar** is a sci-fi film by *Christopher Nolan* about a former NASA pilot who leaves his kids behind to find humanity a new home as Earth dies.

It's ~~just a space movie~~ really a story about a father and his daughter. More on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_(film)).
